Songs from

These are Spotify's three listening windows; there's no true all-time option. Playlists hold 1 to 50 songs per person. Shared songs (liked by everyone first, then by fewer people) take up to 1/3 of the playlist for 2 people, 1/2 for 3, 2/3 for 4 and 4/5 for 5. The rest is split evenly. Slots that don't divide evenly go to a shared song, or the playlist is slightly shorter.
